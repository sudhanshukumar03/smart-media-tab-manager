Manual Mode MANUAL

In Manual Mode, switching to a tab that isn't marked as music or lecture pauses both. Default: off (music keeps playing).

Audio

Ramps volume over ~500ms when pausing/resuming — less jarring than an instant cut. The Layer 2 mute fallback stays instant regardless.

Notifications

Brief system notification each time playback switches. Default: on. Also used for same-category conflict alerts (e.g. two music tabs playing at once).
